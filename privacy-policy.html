Privacy Policy

How SankiCoder and the ColorVilla and PomoPulse Chrome extensions collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Applies to sankicoder.com and the ColorVilla and PomoPulse Chrome extensions

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and the choices you have over it. It covers the SankiCoder website (sankicoder.com) and two Chrome extensions distributed through the Chrome Web Store: ColorVilla (a color-picking tool) and PomoPulse (a Pomodoro timer with ambient soundscapes and streak tracking).

By using our website, submitting the contact form, subscribing to the newsletter, or installing ColorVilla or PomoPulse, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Website (sankicoder.com)

  • Contact form: your name, email address, phone number (optional), the service you are interested in, and your project message, when you send us an enquiry.
  • Newsletter signup: your email address, when you subscribe to our updates.

2.2 ColorVilla Chrome extension

  • Welcome page (optional): your name and email address, submitted only if you choose to share them so the developer can send product updates.
  • Local settings: your picked-color history, pinned favorites, and preferred color format are stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local and never leave your device.

2.3 PomoPulse Chrome extension

  • Welcome page (optional): your name and email address, submitted only if you choose to share them so the developer can send product updates.
  • Local timer data: your session lengths, session counts, streak history, and ambient-sound preferences are stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage.local and never leave your device.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, browsing history, bookmarks, or any sensitive categories of personal data.

3. How We Collect and Why

  • To respond to enquiries: the contact form lets us reply about your project. This is the core purpose of the data we ask for.
  • To send updates you requested: newsletter, ColorVilla, and PomoPulse signups only occur when you provide your email.
  • To power the extensions' features: ColorVilla saves picked-color history and favorites locally; PomoPulse saves session counts, streak history, and ambience preferences locally — all so your settings persist across browsing sessions.

5. Where Your Data Goes (Third-Party Processors)

We use Web3Forms (web3forms.com) as our form-to-email service to deliver website enquiries and ColorVilla / PomoPulse signups. When you submit one of these forms, the submitted fields are transmitted to Web3Forms, which delivers them to us by email. Web3Forms acts solely as a data processor on our behalf; its own privacy terms are available at web3forms.com. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

6. Cookies & Analytics

This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics. This is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub stores only standard server access logs, consistent with its own policies). Neither ColorVilla nor PomoPulse makes tracking requests or performs analytics — the only network calls they can make are delivering a name/email you voluntarily submit.

7. Data Retention

  • Website: enquiry details are retained only as long as needed to complete your project discussion; newsletter emails are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Extensions: ColorVilla's color history/favorites and PomoPulse's timer data, streaks, and ambience settings remain on your device until you clear your browser storage or uninstall the extension. Local copies of submitted signup information may exist in the recipient's mailbox and in Web3Forms' systems per their retention policy.

If you'd like your details removed, contact us and we will delete what we hold within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete / erase your data (the "right to be forgotten").
  • Object to certain processing, and not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us (section 10). We will verify your identity before acting on the request.

9. Security & Children

Security: we use reputable processors (such as Web3Forms) that transmit form data over encrypted HTTPS connections, and we limit access to the mailboxes that receive submissions. Both extensions store their local data in Chrome's sandboxed chrome.storage area.

Children: our services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Contact & Complaints

For any questions about this policy or to exercise your data rights, contact us via the contact form on our website. We aim to respond within 24 hours. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you may also complain to your local data protection authority (e.g., your national GDPR supervisory authority).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the latest version. Material changes will be highlighted here; continued use of the website, ColorVilla, or PomoPulse after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Questions? Email us through the contact form, or see the SankiCoder homepage.
